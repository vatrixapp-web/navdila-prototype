GLALA STUDIOS · CONNECTED HEALTHCARE

Healthcare, finally in sync.

One connected platform linking wholesalers, pharmacies, clinics, and hospitals across every step of care.

One ecosystem. Four essential players.

Purpose-built tools, connected by a shared compliance engine.

▦01 / SUPPLY

Wholesalers

GS1 distribution, GDP compliance, cold-chain logs, carton disaggregation, and a B2B credit ledger.

✚02 / DISPENSE

Pharmacies

Retail POS, offline sync, FEFO picking, prescription gate, temperature logs, and inspection reports.

⌕03 / CARE

Clinics

Outpatient EMR, triage, e-prescribing, lab orders, and flexible cash or M-Pesa billing.

▥04 / CONNECT

Hospitals

Bed management, multi-department billing, SHA e-Claims integration, and multi-store pharmacy.

A smarter supply loop

Every connection creates a more reliable healthcare network.

⤳

From low stock to verified delivery

A pharmacy stock alert can trigger an electronic purchase order to a Navdila wholesaler. On delivery, a GS1 DataMatrix scan can populate batch numbers, expiry dates, and stock—without manual entry.

Built to scale with care

A strong foundation for a connected future.

Architectural principles designed to take the platform from six pilot pharmacies to connected hospital networks—without a costly rewrite.

⌘

Universal data schema

A standardized national drug catalogue with nested packaging—from master case to box, inner pack, and tablet—to support both bulk wholesale and piece dispensing.

⇄

E-prescribing interoperability

Clinics and hospitals can send encrypted prescription payloads to the pharmacy module through local WebSocket or sync workflows.

⌖

SHA & DHA alignment

Patient structures designed to align with Kenya's Digital Health Act and FHIR specifications for Social Health Authority (SHA) claims workflows.

≋

Append-only audit engine

Corrections are recorded as new entries with reasons rather than silently overwriting history, supporting tamper-evident audit trails.

Designed for real-world operations

Practical capabilities that keep teams working and records connected.

Work through interruptions

Offline-first pharmacy workflows keep day-to-day operations moving, with sync when connectivity returns.

Trace every package

Nested product units and batch-level records support a clear path from wholesale cartons to dispensed items.

Keep teams accountable

Role-based workflows and durable audit records help teams manage access and compliance across locations.
