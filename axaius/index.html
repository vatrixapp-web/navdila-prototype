Parent Brand Relationship Architecture

Guidelines for positioning AXAIUS as a standalone product endorsed by Glala Studios.

Where to Mention "Glala Studios"
  • Website Footer & About Page: "AXAIUS is developed and maintained by Glala Studios."
  • Investor Decks & B2B Proposals: Use the formal endorsement lockup (*AXAIUS — Developed by Glala Studios*).
  • Legal & Compliance Documents: Software licensing agreements, contracts, and county permit forms.
  • Official Launch Announcements: PR releases and press notes.
Where AXAIUS Stands Alone
  • Daily Pharmacy Counter Interface: Keep UI header clean with just "AXAIUS Health".
  • App Icons & Favicons: Use standalone AXAIUS emblem only.
  • Hardware Decals & Stickers: "Powered by AXAIUS — Always Ahead."
  • Routine Notifications: System emails and WhatsApp alerts.
